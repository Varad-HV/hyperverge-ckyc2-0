    Nothing is left to chance

    Every outcome is handled, and clearly explained.

    Whatever happens during the journey, the customer sees a plain-language screen with a next step, and NIA receives a clear status to act on.

    Approved

    Record retrieved, or new details accepted by the registry. The customer is done.

    • Existing CKYC record downloaded
    • New record submitted
    • Customer already in CKYC at upload

    Needs review

    Something needs a human eye. The customer is told NIA will be in touch.

    • Name does not match the policy
    • An ID could not be verified
    • Consent form not accepted

    Declined

    The customer could not complete verification after the allowed attempts.

    • OTP attempts used up
    • Selfie liveness failed
    • Face details did not match
    Aadhaar consent is enforcedIf a customer does not allow it in DigiLocker, Aadhaar is never fetched. They are offered another way instead.
    Retries before giving upUnreadable photo, DigiLocker hiccup or wrong PAN: the customer gets a clear fix and another go.
    Names are matched, not assumedThe name on the ID, on the policy and in the registry are compared, with spelling tolerance and honorifics ignored.
    Under the hood

    What runs behind each screen.

    The customer sees a few simple screens. Behind them, HyperVerge connects the checks NIA needs for CKYC 2.0, and hands back one clear answer.

    DigiLocker

    Fetches Aadhaar and PAN straight from the government source, with consent enforced.

    Live

    ID reading

    Reads passport, voter ID or driving licence, front and back, and pre-fills the form.

    Live

    Selfie liveness

    Confirms a real person is present: blur, closed eyes, sunglasses and multiple faces are caught.

    Live

    Government databases

    Checks passport (MEA), voter ID (ECI) and driving licence (MoRTH) at the source.

    Live

    Name matching

    Compares policy, ID and registry names with spelling tolerance, ignoring honorifics.

    Live

    PAN verification

    Validates the PAN with NSDL. Configurable, switched off in this demo.
